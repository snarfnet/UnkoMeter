# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

UnkoMeter is a simple health log for toilet duration, stool condition, and optional notes.

## Data We Store

The app stores your records on your device. Records may include:

- Toilet duration
- Stool condition
- Optional notes you type
- Date and time of the record

## Data We Collect

We do not collect your toilet log data.

UnkoMeter does not use accounts or our own servers. Your records stay on your device unless you choose to share them outside the app using your own device features.

## Advertising

UnkoMeter uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner ads. Google may receive device, advertising, and usage information to provide ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Google handles this data under its own policies.

## Health Information

UnkoMeter is not a medical app and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. If you have ongoing symptoms, severe pain, blood in stool, or other health concerns, please contact a medical professional.
